01

The Parties

This Agreement is entered into between Hermia ("Provider", ABN 75 434 177 026) and the Client identified during sign-up. By proceeding, the Client agrees to receive Hermia's AI intake and lead qualification software on a month-to-month subscription basis.

02

Hermia AI Intake & Qualification Software

The Provider delivers AI-powered intake and qualification software designed to engage with the Client's existing inbound enquiries. When a prospective customer submits an enquiry through the Client's connected inbox — Gmail or Outlook — Hermia may automatically collect and organise relevant information — including contact details, service requirements, urgency, supporting media, availability, and other qualification data.

This information is then structured and routed into the Client's connected CRM or business system to reduce manual administration and improve response times.

Price basis

Prices are in USD and include GST. One service and one price apply to every industry; there are no limited/unlimited tiers.

The initial agreed configuration includes up to 35 client-authored questions, supported channels and response routes, internal recipients, photo and video handling, approved calendar steps, and the CRM or email-docket handoff. Support and monitoring are included in the monthly service. There are no separate Hermia fees per question, answer, enquiry, message, platform, recipient, media submission or CRM connection.

After go-live, a requested configuration change costs USD $500 per submitted change request. Several changes submitted together count as one request, including adding, removing or rewording questions, changing recipients, channels, handoff steps or a CRM route. This is not a per-question fee, a minimum fee plus implementation extras, or a recurring surcharge. No fee is added without the Client requesting and approving the work.

The setup fee is paid by card at checkout before the two-day service trial begins. The first USD $1,700 monthly payment is taken at the end of the trial, then recurs monthly on the billing anniversary. The trial waives only the monthly service charge during those two days; it does not waive the setup payment. Prices are in USD; a card provider may apply its own exchange rate or foreign-transaction fee.

If a subscription payment fails, Hermia emails the Client with a payment link. An unpaid account is paused while its setup is retained. Once payment is confirmed and the subscription is active, access is restored automatically without another initial configuration. If the trial ends without an active paid subscription, the service pauses and the Client is notified by email.

These published terms govern new orders and do not retrospectively change existing signed agreements or subscriptions. The configuration agreement records your selected operating instructions before checkout.

04

Refund Policy

If the Client requests cancellation within the two-day trial (48 hours from the setup payment at checkout), the ordinary setup refund is USD $4,316: USD $4,400 less the USD $84 processing deduction. No monthly service payment is taken for a cancellation made within that trial. After the trial, the setup payment is ordinarily non-refundable, because the business configuration has been built and checked.

These commercial refund, cancellation and deduction terms do not exclude, limit or override any mandatory consumer guarantee, statutory refund, compensation or other right or remedy under applicable law. The USD $84 deduction applies only to the ordinary trial-cancellation refund where legally permitted; it is not deducted from a refund where the law requires otherwise.

05

How to Cancel

The service is month-to-month, with no minimum subscription term or long-term lock-in. The Client may cancel at any time by emailing info@hermia.au with the business name and customer ID, or using the billing-portal link in the monthly invoice. Cancellation takes effect immediately: new enquiry processing stops and no further monthly payments are taken. There is no 48-hour renewal-notice requirement. The unused portion of an already paid month is not ordinarily refunded, subject to mandatory legal rights.

The deployed business configuration is retained for up to 12 months after cancellation or while unpaid, without continuing new enquiry processing. After 12 months it is removed; returning after removal requires a rebuild from the saved configuration rather than merely switching the old deployment back on. This setup-retention period is not a promise to retain customer personal data for 12 months; that data remains subject to the Privacy Policy and applicable law.

The Provider maintains commercially reasonable organisational and technical safeguards for the Service. Hermia is designed to support client compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Australian Privacy Principles, and applicable communications rules, but no website, contract, provider, or technical control guarantees compliance with every law or prevents every security incident.

The Client must protect its connected accounts and authorise only lawful workflows. If Hermia communicates with an end customer on the Client’s behalf, the intended disclosure is: “Hi, I’m Hermia, the digital assistant for [Business Name]. I’m collecting a few details so the [Business Name] team can review your request.” The Client remains responsible for the accuracy of the business identity, customer notices, lawful basis, consent evidence, marketing permissions, and channel requirements.

Enquiry Generation Disclaimer

Important

Hermia does not generate, purchase, source, advertise for, or supply leads or customer enquiries. The Client is solely responsible for generating enquiries through its own marketing, advertising, referral, and sales activities.

Hermia's role is limited to processing, qualifying, organising, and routing enquiries already received by the Client. Hermia makes no guarantee regarding enquiry volume, conversion rates, revenue, bookings, or any other business outcome.

08

Enquiry Data & Consent

The Client warrants that it has authority to connect its systems and has provided any notices, lawful basis, or consent required for the collection, use, disclosure, and processing of enquiry data. The Client must keep evidence of consent where required and must not use Hermia to send marketing or telemarketing messages unless the relevant permissions, sender identification, unsubscribe, suppression, and calling controls are in place.

Hermia processes enquiry data only to provide the Service on the Client’s documented instructions and does not use it for Hermia’s independent advertising or lead generation. The Client must promptly tell Hermia about an opt-out, withdrawal, correction request, suspected unauthorised disclosure, or other restriction affecting a person’s data. Hermia may suppress future automated messages and suspend a workflow when required to reduce risk.

Hermia does not record or transcribe private conversations by default. If the Client enables recording, voice-note transcription, or a similar feature, the Client must provide the required notice and obtain any consent required by the applicable state, territory, or country. Health, finance, insurance, legal, employment, education, government, children’s, and other regulated workflows may require additional review, human approval, restricted features, or refusal.

The Client determines the purposes and lawful means of its customer communications and remains responsible for the instructions supplied to Hermia. Where applicable, Hermia acts as a technical processor or service provider, not as the Client’s independent controller of customer relationships. The Client must ensure that its instructions, customer notices, consent records, suppression controls, and cross-border arrangements satisfy the laws that apply to the Client, its customers, and the selected communication channels.
