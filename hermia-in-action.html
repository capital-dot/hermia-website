01 / The conversation

Get past “there’s a leak.”

An incoming message is only a starting point. Hermia follows the questions your business approves, asks for the missing context and keeps the answers with the enquiry, so your office has more than a vague request to work with.

  • Your own questions, in your business’s words.
  • The reported fault, location and availability in one conversation.
  • A clear opt-out route for the person replying.
02 / The CRM record

A brief your office can work with.

The useful detail should not stay buried in a chat. An approved Simpro handoff can carry the reported issue and relevant notes into the record your team uses, ready for a person to review instead of retyping the whole conversation.

  • Keep the customer’s account of the problem attached to the lead.
  • Give descriptions and notes a clear purpose.
  • Let your team assess the work and decide the next step.
03 / The calendar context

Give availability a place to land.

A preferred time is useful only when the right people can see it. In a configured scheduling workflow, customer availability can be captured alongside the enquiry and passed into an approved calendar or schedule for the team to review.

  • Collect the customer’s preferred time.
  • Keep business hours and approved scheduling rules visible.
  • Confirm the booking through the authorised workflow—not by assumption.
04 / The team handoff

The same context. In the right hands.

Your team should not have to piece together a message, an image and an email to understand one enquiry. A prepared docket brings the available detail together and routes it to the recipients your business chooses.

  • A concise reported-issue summary.
  • Supporting evidence kept with the same enquiry.
  • Approved recipients, with access and delivery defined by your business.

01 / The conversation

Your questions. A clearer first brief.

Before a record reaches your AroFlo workflow, the enquiry needs the context your team cares about. Hermia can use your approved question sequence to collect that detail through the connected conversation channel.

  • Choose the questions that matter to your operation.
  • Collect answers rather than asking the office to chase them later.
  • Keep professional judgement and final decisions with your team.
02 / The record destination

Keep your working system. Improve what enters it.

A CRM handoff should fit the fields and record types your people already understand. An AroFlo workflow is mapped to the specific account and approved operating rules, then tested before the business relies on it.

  • Agree how customers, locations and enquiries should be represented.
  • Map the collected answers to the appropriate record fields.
  • Verify the specific connection and handoff before go-live.
03 / The next step

Capture the time. Keep the team in control.

Hermia can collect the customer’s preferred availability and clarify the business’s approved hours. The right next step might be an office review, a callback or an enabled scheduling handoff; your workflow decides.

  • Ask for a practical time instead of leaving it unspoken.
  • Use the business’s approved availability rules.
  • Treat a preferred time as a request until it is confirmed.
04 / The prepared handoff

One enquiry. One useful handoff.

The same prepared detail can support your approved CRM route and the people responsible for acting on it. Your team gets the reported issue, supporting context and unanswered items together rather than spread across different channels.

  • Keep the customer’s original account with the brief.
  • Send the prepared docket to configured recipients.
  • Give the office context for the next conversation.

01 / Your intake standard

Start with what your business needs.

Your industry may be different, but the enquiry still needs context. Start with the questions, channels and handoff your team wants, then scope the actual CRM connection rather than assuming every system works in the same way.

  • Define your enquiry types and approved questions.
  • Choose the channels customers and your team will use.
  • Make the handoff match your operating standard.
02 / Your destination

A clearer brief. Before the next move.

A prepared docket shows the kind of context your team can receive. A direct CRM connection, particular calendar or specialist record type is agreed and tested separately for your system; the example here does not imply that an unlisted CRM is already connected.

  • Agree the destination and permissions.
  • Check the available CRM and calendar capabilities.
  • Review the proposed handoff before it goes live.
