This agreement records the configuration choices you have made for Hermia before payment. It is designed to be clear, fixed, and specific to your operating rules.

01 / Configuration service

Hermia will configure its email enquiry qualification around the operating choices shown below. Hermia qualifies your existing customer enquiries; it does not generate leads. Your business retains control of final commercial decisions, including pricing, estimates, and booking where you select a team handoff.

02 / Fixed configuration price

The configuration is priced at a fixed $800 AUD one-time setup fee and $600 AUD per month. Your selected email inbox, qualification depth, and operating choices do not change this agreed configuration price.

03 / Your operating rules

Hermia will use the approved configuration to ask for relevant job detail, supporting evidence where selected, site and safety context, availability, materials, billing context, and property details. It will not invent a price, estimate, availability, or outcome. Where a response is unclear, it can seek the missing detail before your team receives the prepared enquiry.

04 / Approval & payment

By selecting the confirmation below, you authorise Hermia to record this configuration as your selected operating standard and proceed to secure payment. Your payment receipt and final configuration reference will be associated with this configuration order.
