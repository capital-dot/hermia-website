01 /The parties

This Agreement is entered into between Hermia (Provider, ABN 75 434 177 026) and the Client identified during configuration. By proceeding, the Client agrees to receive Hermia’s month-to-month enquiry qualification service.

02 /Configuration service

Hermia will configure its enquiry qualification around the Client’s selected operating rules. Hermia qualifies the Client’s existing customer enquiries; it does not generate leads. The Client retains control of final commercial decisions, including pricing, estimates, and scheduling where a team handoff is selected.

Where selected, Hermia may collect and organise relevant job information including customer details, service requirements, urgency, supporting evidence, availability, site context, and other approved qualification detail. That information is prepared for the Client’s chosen service system to reduce manual administration and improve response times.

Your approved pricing material remains yours. Hermia never invents or sets prices. Where the Client permits an estimate, it is handled only according to the Client’s approved pricing material, the affected asset type, and the fault description.

03 /Pricing

The Configuration Journey is priced at a fixed $800 AUD one-time setup fee and $600 AUD per month. The Client’s chosen inbox, qualification depth, trades, and operating rules do not change this agreed Configuration price.

04 /Refund policy

All fees paid by the Client are fully refundable within 48 hours of payment, provided a refund is requested within that window.

A non-refundable processing fee of $84 AUD applies to every refund. This amount will be deducted from the refunded total in all cases and is retained by the Provider to cover payment processing and administrative costs, regardless of the reason for cancellation.

No refunds, full or partial, will be issued for requests made more than 48 hours after the original payment date.

05 /How to cancel

This is a month-to-month service with no long-term lock-in. To cancel, the Client may email info@hermia.au with the subject line “Cancel Subscription”, or manage and cancel the subscription through the billing portal link provided in the monthly invoice.

Cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the next billing date to avoid further charges. Refunds for cancellations are subject to Clause 04.

06 /Compliance & security

The Provider maintains communication services in accordance with Australian telecommunications standards and takes commercially reasonable measures to maximise message deliverability. Hermia operates in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

07 /Enquiry generation disclaimer

Hermia does not generate, purchase, source, advertise for, or supply leads or customer enquiries. The Client is solely responsible for generating enquiries through its own marketing, advertising, referral, and sales activities.

Hermia’s role is limited to processing, qualifying, organising, and routing enquiries already received by the Client. Hermia makes no guarantee regarding enquiry volume, conversion rates, revenue, bookings, or any other business outcome.

08 /Enquiry data & consent

The Client warrants that it has obtained all necessary consents from enquirers before passing their contact details to Hermia for processing. The Client is solely responsible for compliance with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Act 1988 in relation to enquirer consent. Hermia processes enquiry data solely on the Client’s instructions and only for delivering the Service.

09 /Intellectual property

Hermia’s conversation flows, qualification logic, triage methodology, and operating system are proprietary to Hermia. The Client may not reproduce, copy, share, reverse engineer, or resell any part of the Hermia service. Breach of this clause may entitle Hermia to immediate termination without refund.

10 /Limitation of liability

Hermia helps qualify and organise enquiries received by the Client. While the Provider strives to provide a reliable service, it does not guarantee lead conversions, sales, bookings, or other business outcomes.

Hermia is not responsible for lost revenue, missed opportunities, or business losses arising from use of the Service. Any liability relating to the Service is limited to the fees paid by the Client in the three months before the claim.
