How Hermia handles
price guidance.

Hermia uses only the material you approve. It can ask a customer about an estimate amount, but it never invents a price, promises a final quote, or changes your commercial rules.

1. Read the job storyHermia collects the asset, fault, urgency and supporting evidence first.
2. Match your guideIt compares those facts with the service, scope and conditions in your approved material.
3. Keep it an estimateAny amount is contextual guidance, not a final quote or a promise to the customer.
4. Escalate uncertaintyIf there is no close fit, Hermia keeps price discussion with your office.
