Question Templates

What should
Hermia ask?

This is the main thing you control. Hermia only ever asks leads questions from pre-approved templates — never freeform AI generation — and every CRM field on the other end is already built in on our side, so this is where your build actually gets shaped. Each template is one specific question, priced at $1,000. Short-form is fine — we word it properly using your business details.

Every template added updates your setup fee in real time.
Pricing Tiers

What should
Hermia quote?

When a lead describes their issue, Hermia matches it against these tiers and gives a real price before the job is booked — worked out from your own rates, not guessed. No cost to add these. If you leave this empty, every price question is handed straight to your negotiation contact instead.

Example: "Burst pipe repair" — $600. Add as many or as few as you like.
