QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING

Know what
comes next.

Can Hermia clear conflicts?

No. A qualified member of the practice must perform its own conflicts process.

Can a client share a confidential file?

Not through an unapproved pilot channel. Document intake, consent and safeguards must be designed and tested with the practice first.
