Trade contractors · current pathways

Know the job before your team acts.

Enquiries from approved inboxes and connected or forwarded sources become structured trade job briefs. Hermia asks follow-ups for the asset, defect, location, urgency, safety and access details instead of treating “the roof is leaking” as a finished job brief.

  • Plumbing, roofing, electrical, remedial and fire/ESM question flows.
  • Existing Simpro and AroFlo handoffs, subject to the specific onboarded integration.
  • Private email dockets for configured recipients; technicians retain on-site judgement, safety assessment, licensed work and final quoting.
Available for scoped trade onboarding. A selected source still needs its actual account connection and tested routing before go-live.

Property & real estate · scoped pilot

Give property enquiries a clear route.

A property team may need to distinguish a maintenance request from a new leasing enquiry, capture the property and access context, then put a concise brief in front of the correct manager. The precise questions, permissions and destination system must be agreed per business.

  • Define property, issue or enquiry type, contact and urgency fields.
  • Route an approved brief to the appropriate operations or property team.
  • Keep tenancy, safety, legal and final scheduling decisions with authorised staff.
Design example, not a claim of a live property-management integration. A client-specific pilot and connector tests are required.

Legal services · scoped pilot

Organise the enquiry without giving advice.

A law practice could collect contact details, matter category, jurisdiction and preferred follow-up route before a qualified person reviews a new enquiry. Confidentiality, conflicts checks and privilege need a practice-specific design.

  • Ask only approved intake questions relevant to the practice.
  • Separate routine administration from urgent review requests.
  • Never represent AI intake as legal advice or conflict clearance.
Proposed use case only. No live legal case-management connector or compliance certification is implied.

Healthcare administration · scoped pilot

Support intake. Leave care with clinicians.

A healthcare organisation may want to organise administrative enquiries, appointment requests and missing paperwork before a trained team member follows up. Clinical triage, protected health information and safety escalation require a separate specialist implementation.

  • Start with low-risk, non-clinical administrative workflows.
  • Define consent, sensitive-data handling and escalation with the organisation.
  • Do not make diagnoses, clinical triage decisions or care recommendations.
Concept only. No live healthcare integration or regulated-data readiness is claimed.

Finance operations · scoped pilot

Make document follow-up easier to action.

For an approved workflow, a team could identify which expected document or form field is missing, ask the customer to correct it, and record a brief for human review. The document rules and delivery channels must be designed and tested with the institution.

  • Define a narrow document checklist and approved follow-up wording.
  • Escalate exceptions and sensitive cases to authorised staff.
  • Never claim loan decisions, financial advice, underwriting or a live banking connector.
Concept only. Salesforce, bank systems and regulated document processing are not connected by this website.

Insurance companies · scoped pilot

Prepare a claim enquiry for the right person.

An insurer may receive a claim reference, incident account and supporting material in separate messages. A proposed administrative workflow could ask for the missing non-decision details and prepare a brief for authorised claims staff, using the insurer’s approved secure channels.

  • Differentiate a new enquiry from an existing claim follow-up.
  • Collect only approved reference and reported-incident context.
  • Leave coverage, liability, payout and underwriting decisions to authorised staff.
Concept only. No claims platform, insurer account or policy system is connected by this website.
