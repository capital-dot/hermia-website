BUILT FOR THE WORK THAT ALREADY COMES IN

It was never about getting leads.
It's about knowing the work before anyone acts.

Enquiries reach a business as forwarded alerts, half-finished forms, messages, documents and photos. Hermia helps ask what is missing, keep those answers with the original request and prepare a useful brief for the right people. Your team still makes the safety, service and commercial decisions.

A MESSY START / A CLEARER HANDOFF

Operational detail arrives
in pieces.

Hermia helps manage messy operational data: an inbox alert may carry a customer name but no site; a message may describe a problem but omit access, urgency or supporting material. A configured workflow gathers those pieces through approved questions, flags what remains unknown and sends a structured summary to the designated team.

HOW IT WORKS

Operational data in.
A CRM that works.

A CRM is only as useful as what goes into it. Hermia takes the scattered detail your business receives, completes it, and writes it in as a record your team can act on straight away.

Your team stays in control of every decision. Each workflow is configured and tested for your business.
  1. 01

    Reads every enquiry

    Emails, messages, web forms and forwarded alerts, understood in context.

  2. 02

    Asks what’s missing

    One question at a time. Vague answers are challenged, not accepted.

  3. 03

    Writes it into your CRM

    Fields completed, and existing customers matched before anything new is created.

  4. 04

    Tells the right people

    Each nominated person receives their own private copy of the brief.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Clear scope.
No guesswork.

Does Hermia bring my business new customers?

No. Hermia works on the enquiries your business already receives, through sources you approve and connect.

Can we choose our own questions and recipients?

Yes. You choose them during configuration, and each one is built and tested for your business before it goes live.

Which CRMs and industries are available today?

Each industry page sets out what is live now, which CRMs are supported and what is still a scoped pilot.

Does Hermia make decisions for my team?

No. Your authorised people stay responsible for bookings, pricing, safety and any legal, clinical, financial or insurance judgement.
