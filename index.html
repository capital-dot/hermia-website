BUILT FOR THE WORK THAT ALREADY COMES IN

It was never about getting leads.
It's about knowing the work before anyone acts.

Enquiries reach a business as forwarded alerts, half-finished forms, messages, documents and photos. Hermia helps ask what is missing, keep those answers with the original request and prepare a useful brief for the right people. Your team still makes the safety, service and commercial decisions.

A MESSY START / A CLEARER HANDOFF

Operational detail arrives
in pieces.

Hermia helps manage messy operational data: an inbox alert may carry a customer name but no site; a message may describe a problem but omit access, urgency or supporting material. A configured workflow gathers those pieces through approved questions, flags what remains unknown and sends a structured summary to the designated team.

HOW IT WORKS

Not just a chatbot.
A controlled intake system.

This is the operating pattern behind a configured workflow. Questions, sources and destinations have to be approved, connected and tested for each business.

01 / INTAKE

Start with the enquiry
you already have.

Use a connected inbox, approved message channel or forwarded alert to bring a request into its intended path. The business decides which sources belong in scope. Hermia is not a marketplace and does not find or buy the lead.

KEEP THE ORIGINAL CONTEXT
  • The reported issue or request
  • Who is asking and how to reply
  • Site, property, matter or reference context where appropriate
  • Attachments or photos only through approved channels
02 / QUALIFICATION

Ask the next useful question.
Not a generic one.

A supported trade flow checks whether an answer contains usable detail and asks again when it is too broad. A business can request changes to its question path; those changes are recorded for an approved client-specific build, then validated before they are made active. Regulated-sector examples on this site are proposals, not working question sets.

03 / HANDOFF

Send the context
to the right people.

A completed trade brief can reach configured team members as separate private email dockets. Supported Simpro or AroFlo handoffs are set up and tested for the contractor's specific workflow. Staff control booking, safety, licensed work, pricing and final service decisions unless a particular approved action is explicitly connected and tested.

THE HANDOFF MAY INCLUDE
  • What was reported—and what is still unknown
  • Relevant asset, location, access and timing notes
  • Approved media or reference links where present
  • Private recipient routing, not visible CC lists
04 / CLIENT-SPECIFIC DESIGN

The same standard.
Different questions.

The business selects the questions and destinations that suit its operation. New configurations and change requests are recorded for a client-specific build; a recorded request is not an instantly active messaging flow. Unsupported industries, CRMs and channels must first be scoped and connected, then tested before anyone promises launch.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Clear scope.
No guesswork.

Does Hermia bring my business new customers?

No. Hermia helps qualify enquiries your business already receives through its approved, connected or forwarded sources.

Can we use our own questions and routes?

You can request them through the trade configuration. Your choices are recorded for a client-specific build, and a new question or channel must be implemented and tested before it becomes active.

Is every industry or CRM connected today?

No. The paid pathway is currently scoped to supported trades with Simpro or AroFlo. Other industry workflows, destinations and messaging accounts require a separate pilot and verification.

Does Hermia decide the job, diagnosis or claim?

No. The authorised team remains responsible for safety, on-site assessment, booking, quotes and all legal, clinical, financial or insurance decisions.

Where are the current trade prices?

See the Trades plans for the published AUD Limited and Unlimited figures. A custom configuration has a separate selection-based estimate and server-authoritative checkout.
