01 / WHAT HERMIA DOES

Not another inbox.
A way through it.

Hermia is an AI-assisted conversation and handoff system. It works to a business-approved question path, not a one-size-fits-all script. When an answer is too broad, it asks for the missing detail instead of pretending the brief is complete.

01 / RECEIVE

Meet the enquiry.

Connected or forwarded messages can enter an approved intake path. Each source needs its own connection and test.

02 / CLARIFY

Ask what matters.

Follow-ups can request the asset, symptom, location, access or evidence the team actually needs.

03 / HAND OFF

Give context to people.

Prepare a private brief for configured recipients and, on supported trade builds, a connected CRM handoff.

In a connected workflow, the conversation can continue by email or WhatsApp. New questions, channels and destinations are connected and tested before they go live. Hermia does not make safety assessments or final decisions.

03 / WHERE PEOPLE STAY IN CHARGE

The intelligence helps.
Your team decides.

Hermia prepares context. Technicians, clinicians, lawyers, financial professionals and operations staff keep responsibility for their own decisions. Hermia does not replace on-site judgement, safety assessment, licensed work, a final quote, legal advice, clinical care or financial advice.

Current paid configuration is limited to supported Simpro and AroFlo trade workflows. Other industries require a separately scoped build and verified connections before use.
