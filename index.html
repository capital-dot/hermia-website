How It Works

From a vague enquiry
to a usable job brief.

Hermia keeps qualifying incomplete messages until your office or technician has the detail needed to decide the next step. It prepares the context; your team stays in control of the decision.

01 — Qualification

Vague enquiry in.
Usable job brief out.

“It’s leaking”, “the roof” or “there is a defect” is not enough for your team to act on. Hermia keeps the conversation focused until the problem is clear.

It asks trade-specific questions to pin down the item, the issue, the affected area, urgency, access, safety and useful evidence. If the answer is still too vague, Hermia keeps qualification open instead of handing your team a guess.

What your team receives
The point: your office and technicians receive a clear starting point, not another vague message to decode.
05 — Site Address & Availability

The right site, the right address, the right access window.

Before a job is sent to the calendar, Hermia asks for the full work address and confirms it back to the lead. This stops an incomplete street name or a vague building reference becoming the job location.

It then asks when someone can provide access and corrects availability that falls outside your configured visit window. The booking only continues once the site and time are clear enough to schedule.

Address confirmation in action
The lead confirms the full address, then selects a real visit time that your business can actually service.
06 — Pricing

A real estimate — from your rates, not a guess.

During onboarding, you set your pricing criteria — what a defect rectification, an inspection, or a callout roughly costs at your rates. When a qualified lead describes their situation, Hermia matches it against your tiers and gives them a real figure before the job is booked. The "how much roughly?" conversation happens without touching your office — and price shoppers reveal themselves before costing you a cent of admin.

If they accept: the agreed figure is logged on the job record — no confusion when the job hits your schedule. If they negotiate: they're handed straight to the negotiation contact you chose at onboarding, and you're told they're reaching out about pricing before they contact you. The job lands in your CRM either way.

Estimate before booking
The estimate is shown before the job progresses, using the pricing rules you provide.
Inside Simpro

One qualified lead.
Three Simpro modules, already organised.

Hermia is set up here for Simpro only. Each qualified enquiry is separated into the Customer, Custom Site and Open Lead records your trade office actually works from — so the job is usable before anyone starts retyping.

01 — Simpro Customers

The right customer,
before another record is made.

Hermia checks the customer first using the contact information provided through qualification. That means a returning property manager, landlord or homeowner is linked back to the correct Simpro customer instead of being entered again under a slightly different name.

For trade businesses, this protects customer history, billing relationships and repeat-work context. Your team can see who the customer is before they decide what needs to happen next.

02 — Simpro Custom Sites

Not a loose address.
A custom site your crew can use.

Hermia places the work location into a dedicated Simpro Site record beneath the right customer. It confirms the actual site before booking so a client with multiple buildings, units or managed properties does not send a technician to the wrong place.

For trades, that keeps the address, access information and building context where the crew needs it. A new address creates a new Custom Site; an existing address stays linked to its existing site history.

03 — Simpro Open Leads

The qualified job.
Visible before your office calls.

Once the customer and Custom Site are right, Hermia creates or enriches the Open Lead with the actual trade detail: what is wrong, how urgent it is, which defect flag applies, what must be isolated, and the evidence the lead supplied.

For trades, the office can see the job context before dispatching anyone. The technician is not walking into a vague message; the lead is already a structured Simpro record with the information needed to action it.
