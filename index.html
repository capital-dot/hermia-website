01 / THE SYSTEM

Less chasing.
More knowing.

Messages, forms and forwarded alerts rarely contain the whole story. Hermia follows the questions a business approves, checks incomplete replies and brings the useful details together before the next handoff.

01

Receive

An enquiry enters through a connected or forwarded source.

02

Clarify

Hermia asks targeted follow-ups. An incomplete answer stays open for clarification.

03

Hand off

Approved context reaches the configured team or connected system for human review.

Each source, question path and destination is configured and tested before use. Selecting a channel does not connect an account automatically.
02 / WHERE IT CAN FIT

Different work.
The same need for clarity.

Hermia is not defined by one industry. The same principle—understand the request, clarify what matters, and pass it to the right people—can be designed around different operations. Availability depends on the specific workflow being built and tested.

03 / WHAT IT CHANGES

Less noise for the team.
A better experience for the customer.

On a connected pathway, Hermia can continue the conversation by email or WhatsApp, ask for the details or evidence your team needs, and prepare a private brief for the people you choose. Supported trade builds can also hand the information into the connected CRM.

01

Meet requests where they arrive

Bring connected inboxes, messaging and forwarded alerts into a clear intake path instead of making the team piece together scattered messages.

02

Ask what is missing

Follow up on vague answers with the questions that matter to the configured workflow, so people are not handed a brief that only says “something is wrong.”

03

Give the right people context

Prepare a readable summary for approved team recipients, with a connected CRM handoff where the customer's supported pathway has been set up.

04

Work to your rules

Choose the information to collect, where the conversation continues and who receives the result. New questions or channels are connected and tested before they go live.

04 / BUILT WITH BOUNDARIES

Your process.
Human judgement.

Hermia can be designed around different teams and types of enquiries. Today, the configured production onboarding path is for trade businesses using supported Simpro or AroFlo workflows. Other industries and integrations are scoped pilots until their connections, safeguards and handoffs are built and tested.

Hermia prepares context. Your people remain responsible for decisions, safety, licensed work and final quotes; it does not provide legal, clinical or financial advice.
