01 / THE PLATFORM

An enquiry is a beginning.
Not a finished brief.

Enquiries rarely arrive ready to act on. Hermia helps a business clarify the requests it already receives: approved questions draw out what is missing, keep the context together and prepare a brief for the people who decide what happens next. Current paid onboarding is scoped to supported trade pathways; other industries need their own approved build and test.

01 / RECEIVE

Listen where the work begins.

Connected inboxes, forwarded alerts and approved channels feed a configured intake path. Each source needs a real connection and an end-to-end test.

02 / QUALIFY

Ask a better next question.

Hermia can ask for the missing asset, issue, location, access, timing or supporting material. Overly broad answers are clarified; uncertain cases stay uncertain.

03 / HAND OVER

Give people the whole picture.

A structured brief can reach designated staff privately and, on supported trade builds, the connected field-service workflow. The team retains the decision.

WHAT THE SYSTEM CHANGES

Less decoding.
More deciding.

One useful story from scattered messages: what was reported, what is known, what still needs checking, and who should see it next. Hermia is not a source of leads, a final decision-maker or a substitute for professional judgement.

03 / INTELLIGENCE WITH BOUNDARIES

Make room for judgement.
Not a replacement for it.

The business chooses the questions, routing and people responsible for follow-up. Hermia prepares the context; a technician, property manager or qualified professional remains responsible for decisions within their field.

It does not make a diagnosis, safety determination, final quote, legal opinion, care decision or financial recommendation. New custom questions and connections go live only after an approved build and test.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you decide.

Does Hermia bring us new customers?

No. It helps your business qualify enquiries it already receives through its approved, connected sources. It does not buy, sell or generate leads.

Is every industry ready to connect now?

No. The current paid configuration pathway supports scoped trade workflows using Simpro or AroFlo. Property, legal, healthcare administration, finance and insurance examples require separate scoping, suitable permissions, safeguards and connector testing before any launch.

Can I choose my own questions or channels?

You can specify your preferred questions, response channels and handoffs in the trade configuration. Those choices are recorded for a client-specific build; selecting them does not instantly create a working connection or activate a new question.

Who makes the final decision?

Your authorised team. Hermia prepares and routes information; staff keep professional judgement, safety, scheduling, pricing and final service decisions.
