It was never about getting leads.
It's about knowing the work before anyone acts.

Enquiries reach a business as forwarded alerts, half-finished forms, messages, documents and photos. Hermia helps ask what is missing, keep those answers with the original request and prepare a useful brief for the right people. Your team still makes the safety, service and commercial decisions.

A MESSY START / A CLEARER HANDOFF

Operational detail arrives
in pieces.

Hermia helps manage messy operational data: an inbox alert may carry a customer name but no site; a message may describe a problem but omit access, urgency or supporting material. A configured workflow gathers those pieces through approved questions, flags what remains unknown and sends a structured summary to the designated team.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU START

Clear scope.
No guesswork.

Does Hermia bring my business new customers?

No. Hermia helps qualify enquiries your business already receives through its approved, connected or forwarded sources.

Can we use our own questions and routes?

You can request them through the trade configuration. Your choices are recorded for a client-specific build, and a new question or channel must be implemented and tested before it becomes active.

Is every industry or CRM connected today?

No. The paid pathway is currently scoped to supported trades with Simpro or AroFlo. Other industry workflows, destinations and messaging accounts require a separate pilot and verification.

Does Hermia decide the job, diagnosis or claim?

No. The authorised team remains responsible for safety, on-site assessment, booking, quotes and all legal, clinical, financial or insurance decisions.

Where are the current trade prices?

See the Trades plans for the published AUD Limited and Unlimited figures. A custom configuration has a separate selection-based estimate and server-authoritative checkout.
