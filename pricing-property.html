02 / YOUR QUESTIONS

The price

One commercial model, whatever your industry.

How much does Hermia cost?

USD $4,400 once for setup, then USD $1,700 a month per business. Prices include GST. There is one standard service, not a menu of plans.

The only optional service charge is USD $500 for a change request you choose to submit after your setup is live. A USD $84 processing deduction applies to an ordinary setup refund during the trial.

Is the price the same for every industry and business size?

Yes. Trades, property, legal, healthcare, finance and insurance use the same setup and monthly price per business. It does not increase with your enquiry volume, connected channels or the people you choose to alert.

Your initial configuration includes up to 35 of your own questions. There is no separate charge for each included question or for the answers customers send.

Are there plans, tiers or a cut-down version?

No. There is one Hermia service and one price per business. You do not choose between limited and unlimited plans, and there is no cheaper version with essential features removed.

Are there hidden charges or usage add-ons?

No Hermia fee is charged per enquiry, message, channel, question, alert recipient or CRM connection. Your initial configuration choices—including up to 35 questions—are covered by the setup fee; the agreed live service is covered by the monthly fee.

Post-live question changes are covered by one USD $500 bundled change request, not a fee for each question. The other separate amount is the USD $84 trial-refund processing deduction; it is not a monthly or usage charge.

What currency are the prices in?

All Hermia prices are in US dollars (USD), for businesses in every country. If your card uses another currency, your bank or card provider converts the payment at its own rate.

Any currency-conversion or foreign-transaction fee imposed by your bank is outside Hermia’s price and is controlled by that provider.

Does the displayed price include GST?

Yes. The displayed Hermia prices include GST: USD $4,400 setup and USD $1,700 per month are the full stated service prices, not amounts with GST added later.

Why are setup, monthly service and changes priced separately?

Setup pays for building and checking your business’s configuration once. The monthly fee pays for running, monitoring and supporting it after it goes live.

Changes are separate because a business that keeps its configuration should not pay for another business’s frequent changes. There is no per-enquiry fee, so a busier week does not create a larger Hermia usage bill.

03 / YOUR QUESTIONS

What is included

Know what your setup and monthly payments cover.

What does the one-off setup fee pay for?

Your own business configuration: the channels enquiries arrive from, the channel used to reply, your questions, where answers belong, your next step, alert recipients and your CRM or other approved delivery destination.

Your configured setup is used only for your business, not shared with another business. Each configured part is checked before enquiries are routed to it, and you receive your customer ID. The initial build is paid for once.

What does the monthly fee cover?

The monthly price covers the agreed live service, support and monitoring, including nights and weekends—not a separate bill for each processed enquiry.

  • Configured replies and your questions, asked one at a time.
  • Two independent review checks on each answer, with another question when an answer is not usable.
  • Photo and message checks, duplicate removal and clearly identified missing details.
  • One configured follow-up when a customer goes quiet, scheduled between 9am and 7pm in your business’s time zone.
  • Delivery of every finished enquiry through the agreed CRM or job-system connection, or a lead-docket email with an owner’s WhatsApp alert.
  • Queuing during busy periods so requests await processing rather than being discarded, daily configuration checks and alerts to our team when a person needs to review something.

The typical reply target is around one minute. Timing and delivery depend on the configured workflow, review steps and channel availability; it is not a guarantee of identical response times or error-free results.

Which channels and notifications are included?

Your approved setup can include email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Google Business notifications, web forms and marketplace notifications. There is no separate Hermia channel or alert-recipient fee.

The actual connections must be supported, available and authorised by your business and the relevant platform. Your questions, photo/message handling, CRM-or-email delivery, owner alert, monitoring and support are included in the agreed configuration.

How many questions can we configure, and are customer answers charged?

You can configure up to 35 of your own questions, in your own words, as part of the initial setup. There is no per-question setup surcharge and no charge each time a customer answers.

Adding, removing or rewording questions after your configuration is live is a change request. Several changes submitted together count as one USD $500 request, not a separate fee for each question in that request.

04 / YOUR QUESTIONS

Billing and the trial

See the sequence before you enter payment details.

When exactly am I charged?

For example, if you sign up on 1 March:

  1. 1 MarchPay the USD $4,400 setup fee at checkout; your card is kept on file.
  2. Next 48 hoursYour two-day service trial. No monthly service fee is charged during this period.
  3. 3 MarchIf you continue, the first USD $1,700 monthly payment is taken when the trial ends.
  4. 3 AprilThe next USD $1,700 payment, followed by monthly payments on the same billing cycle.

This is a billing illustration. Your actual checkout confirmation should show the trial end and next payment date for your account.

How do I pay?

Pay by card through the secure checkout. The card kept on file is used for the recurring monthly service payment. Your payment confirmation identifies the amounts and billing schedule.

Do I pay anything during the two-day trial?

Yes—the USD $4,400 setup fee is paid at checkout. The two-day trial is free of the monthly service fee; it does not mean the initial setup payment is waived.

If you continue beyond the trial, the USD $1,700 monthly fee begins. If you cancel within the trial, the ordinary setup refund is reduced by the USD $84 processing deduction.

When does the monthly fee start?

The first USD $1,700 payment is taken at the end of the two-day trial if you continue. After that, it is billed monthly on the account’s billing cycle. No monthly fee is taken when you cancel within the trial.

06 / YOUR QUESTIONS

Change requests

Initial choices are included. Later changes are separate.

What counts as a USD $500 change request?

A requested change after your setup is live, such as:

  • Adding, removing or rewording your questions.
  • Adding a channel or changing the channel customers receive replies on.
  • Changing where an answer goes or the next step in the enquiry.
  • Changing who receives alerts or delivery notifications.
  • Moving to a different CRM or approved record destination.

Several changes submitted together count as one USD $500 request. This is not a charge for customer answers or a per-question processing fee.

Can we choose all our channels, questions and recipients at setup?

Yes. Those initial choices form your configuration and are included in the setup price. They are not charged as individual add-ons.

Adding or changing them after go-live is a USD $500 change request. Send the related changes together if you want them handled as one request.

How do we submit and pay for a change?

Send the requested changes with your customer ID and pay the USD $500 request fee when you submit the request. Clearly list all the changes you want together so they are treated as one request.

You can contact Hermia to submit the details and arrange the request payment. The requested change and affected configuration should be clear before work begins.

07 / YOUR QUESTIONS

Refunds and cancelling

The trial, deductions and cancellation terms in one place.

How long is the service trial?

The trial lasts two calendar days (48 hours). In the billing sequence above, it begins with your setup payment at checkout and ends before the first monthly service payment.

To cancel during the trial, contact Hermia within that window with your business details and customer ID or payment reference.

What happens if we cancel during the trial?

The ordinary setup refund is USD $4,316: your USD $4,400 setup payment less the USD $84 processing deduction. No monthly service payment is taken.

USD $4,400−USD $84=USD $4,316

Ask to cancel within the two-day trial and keep your request or confirmation for your records. Mandatory legal rights are not excluded.

Why is USD $84 retained from a trial refund?

The USD $84 processing amount covers the cost of processing the ordinary trial refund. It is retained from the setup payment rather than added as another monthly fee.

This commercial refund policy does not remove any refund or remedy required by applicable law.

Can the setup payment be refunded after the trial?

The ordinary setup-fee refund applies only when cancellation is requested within the two-day trial. After that window, the setup fee is not refunded under this commercial policy.

This does not restrict mandatory rights where the law requires a refund or another remedy.

Is there a lock-in contract or minimum subscription term?

No. After the trial, the service is month to month. You can cancel at any time; the annual budgeting examples do not commit you to an annual contract.

What happens when we cancel after the trial?

Hermia stops immediately and no further monthly service payments are taken. The unused portion of a month you have already paid for is not ordinarily refunded.

Your setup is retained for 12 months after cancellation, then removed. This refers to the business configuration; it is not a promise that every enquiry or CRM record is kept for 12 months.

Mandatory legal refund and remedy rights remain available. Contact Hermia with your customer ID to arrange cancellation.

08 / YOUR QUESTIONS

Missed payments

Understand pausing, restoration and retained setup.

What happens if a payment fails?

We email you straight away with a link to pay. If the account remains unpaid, Hermia pauses new enquiry responses and preparation, while your setup is retained.

Once payment is confirmed, the service is restored automatically without another initial configuration. After 12 months unpaid, the deployed setup is removed. Returning after removal requires rebuilding from the saved configuration rather than simply switching the old build back on.

What if the trial ends without an active subscription?

Hermia pauses and you are notified by email. The service does not continue processing new enquiries without an active paid subscription.

09 / YOUR QUESTIONS

With or without a CRM

The connection changes the destination, not the price.

Do we need a CRM, and does connecting one cost extra?

No CRM is required, and there is no separate Hermia CRM-connection fee. The setup and monthly price are the same either way.

With an approved supported connection, prepared enquiries can reach your CRM or job system. Without a CRM, the configured handoff is a secure lead-docket email and an owner’s WhatsApp alert.

If our CRM is not connected yet, do we still pay the full price?

Yes. The same price applies. The configured lead-docket email and owner’s WhatsApp alert provide the handoff while the CRM connection is unavailable.

Do not assume every CRM is already supported. Agree the available connection and delivery method when you configure your business; using the non-CRM delivery path does not reduce the monthly fee.
