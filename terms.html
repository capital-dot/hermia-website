What Hermia Actually Does

Hermia is a CRM-agnostic intake and workflow service: we help businesses read, qualify, organise, and route enquiries or operational requests they already receive. We do not find customers for you, run ads, or guarantee sales, bookings, revenue, or outcomes. Hermia can be configured for different industries, workflows, communication channels, and CRM platforms.

We do this by connecting to two kinds of places:

  • Your email inbox (Gmail or Outlook) — we read new enquiries as they land.
  • Your connected CRM or business system, where enabled — we use it according to the agreed workflow and supported connection.

The sources and steps used depend on your approved configuration. If a request needs follow-up, Hermia may use an enabled channel such as email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or another supported connection before returning the information to your nominated team or CRM.

Where Hermia communicates with an end customer on your behalf, the service should identify both the technology and the business. The standard disclosure is: “Hi, I’m Hermia, the digital assistant for [Business Name]. I’m collecting a few details so the [Business Name] team can review your request.” Hermia must not present itself as a human or as an independent professional adviser.

Client-approved workflows must distinguish an operational reply to an enquiry from marketing. The Client must provide a lawful basis for the relevant collection, use, disclosure, and channel, keep evidence of consent where required, honour withdrawal and opt-out requests, and ensure that any marketing message identifies the Client and includes a usable unsubscribe method. Hermia may suspend or refuse a workflow that appears unlawful, unsafe, misleading, or outside the approved scope.

The Client is the party that decides why enquiry data is collected and how it is used. Hermia acts only on the Client’s documented instructions as a technical service provider or processor where that concept applies. The Client must provide accurate instructions, maintain required notices and consent records, keep suppression lists current, and ensure that every connected channel and recipient is authorised under the law that applies to the Client and its customers.

⚑ Important

Hermia is not a lead generation service. You are solely responsible for marketing, advertising, and bringing in enquiries. We only process enquiries that have already reached your inbox or CRM — we don't create or source them.
