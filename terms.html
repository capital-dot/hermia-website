What Hermia Actually Does

Hermia is a simple job: we read enquiries you already have, and work out which ones are real, qualified jobs. We don't find you customers. We don't run ads. We don't generate leads. You already have enquiries coming in somewhere — we read them, check they're genuine, and hand back the ones worth following up.

We do this by connecting to two kinds of places:

  • Your email inbox (Gmail or Outlook) — we read new enquiries as they land.
  • Your CRM (e.g. SimPro), where connected — we check it too, as a backstop, so nothing slips through if it only shows up there and not in the inbox.

Neither one is the "main" source and neither is optional filler — we check both, because relying on only email or only a CRM means things get missed. If a qualified enquiry needs a follow-up conversation, we may also run that conversation over WhatsApp before handing the details back to you.

⚑ Important

Hermia is not a lead generation service. You are solely responsible for marketing, advertising, and bringing in enquiries. We only process enquiries that have already reached your inbox or CRM — we don't create or source them.
