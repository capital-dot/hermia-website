Can it connect to our property platform now?

Not by selecting this page. We would first scope the platform, permissions and workflow, then test the connection before representing it as available.

Can it decide whether a request is urgent?

It can collect reported urgency and flag an approved escalation path in a pilot. Staff remain responsible for judging and handling emergencies.
