Your plan follows your enquiries

Up when you grow. Down when you don't.

You never have to watch your numbers or ask for a better price. Hermia counts your enquiries every month and moves your plan both ways, and it always tells you first, with the numbers.

One busy month

Nothing changes.

We let you know you were over your plan that month, and that's all. Hermia never stops answering because you're busy.

Over your plan 4 months in a row

Up, with the numbers.

We email you each month's total, where your enquiries came from, and a private list of every enquiry we counted. Your new monthly price starts on your first bill at least 7 days after that email. There's no setup fee for the move.

Under your plan 4 months in a row

Down, by itself.

Your plan moves down on your next bill, to the plan that fits the busiest of those 4 months. You don't have to ask, and we email you the numbers.

What counts as an enquiry

One new person, counted once.

Your plan is based only on how many people get in touch, never on how many messages, questions or replies it takes.

  • One new person getting in touch is one enquiry, whichever way they reach you: an email, or a Facebook, Instagram or TikTok message.
  • If the same person gets in touch again within 7 days, it still counts once.
  • Spam that Hermia blocks isn't counted.
  • Months are calendar months in your own time zone. The month your Hermia goes live is a part month and doesn't count.
  • Your plan can't move until you've had 4 full months live, because there aren't 4 months to go on before then.
  • Every plan email shows your numbers and links to a private list of every enquiry we counted: each person's name, email or phone, the date and the channel. It opens only with your customer ID, and names and contact details are deleted after 6 months.
What the setup pays for

Your own Hermia, built for you alone.

Your enquiries are never dropped into a shared queue. The setup fee builds a dedicated system for your business, from the choices you make when you configure it, and nothing is routed to it until every check has passed. It's usually ready within 1 business day, often much sooner.

  • Where your enquiries come from: email, and Facebook, Instagram and TikTok messages. Lead forms and missed calls are coming soon.
  • The one channel your customers are answered on: WhatsApp or email
  • Up to 35 of your own questions, in your words, alongside the ones Hermia suggests for your industry
  • Where every answer belongs: the description or the notes
  • Who receives each finished lead, by email
  • Your CRM or job system, or the lead docket by email if you don't use one
  • Every part checked, step by step, before it goes live
  • Your customer ID, the reference for anything you change later
What the monthly fee pays for

Every enquiry, every day.

The monthly fee runs everything your Hermia does, for every enquiry, every day of the month, including nights, weekends and your busiest week of the year.

  • A reply in about a minute, day or night, as the assistant for your business
  • Your questions asked one at a time, and asked again when an answer isn't usable
  • Every answer judged by two independent reviewers before it is saved
  • Every photo checked against the enquiry, and every message checked before it is sent
  • One friendly follow-up when a customer goes quiet, at a sensible hour
  • Duplicates removed, so one enquiry becomes one record
  • Every finished enquiry delivered to your CRM or job system, and by email to the people you choose
  • Your setup checked every day, a queue for busy days, and our team alerted the moment something needs a person
Why it's priced this way

Every choice in this price has a reason.

Pricing is a promise about how we will treat your business. These are the reasons behind ours, in plain terms.

01

Plans by enquiries, because that's the work.

Every plan gets the same Hermia: the same checks, the same two reviewers, the same reliability. What changes is how much work it does, and the clearest measure of that is how many people get in touch. So that's the only thing the plans differ by.

02

Your plan comes down by itself.

Most services wait for you to notice you're paying too much. If your enquiries stay under your plan for 4 months in a row, Hermia moves you to the cheaper plan on your next bill and shows you the numbers. You never have to ask.

03

The numbers first, before it goes up.

One busy month never changes your price. Your plan moves up only when you've been over it 4 months in a row, and we email you every month's total and the list of enquiries we counted before the new price starts. There's no setup fee for the move.

04

A setup fee, because yours is built for you.

Your Hermia is a dedicated system, not a login to a shared one. Building it from your configuration and checking every part before it goes live is work done once, so it's paid once. Moving to another plan later changes only the monthly fee.

05

Changes priced on their own, so the monthly fee stays flat.

A change is real work on your own setup. Pricing each kind of change on its own means a business that rarely changes isn't paying for one that changes every week, and the monthly fee stays the same for everyone on the same plan.

06

A free trial that starts when Hermia does.

Your 2 days start the day your Hermia is ready, not the day you pay, so you see it work on your own enquiries before the monthly fee starts. If it isn't right for you, cancel within the 2 days and the setup fee comes back, less the USD 84 it costs to process the refund.

Questions

What businesses ask before they start.

Straight answers about the plans, the trial and the bill.

Which plan should I choose?

The one that fits the enquiries you get in a typical month: Starter up to 300, Standard 301 to 1,000, Growth 1,001 to 2,500, and Large for more than 2,500. Every plan gets the same Hermia.

If you choose wrong, your plan corrects itself from your real numbers, and you're told before anything changes.

What counts as an enquiry?

One new person getting in touch, whichever way they reach you: an email, or a message on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok. If the same person gets in touch again within 7 days, it still counts once. Spam that Hermia blocks isn't counted.

Months are calendar months in your own time zone, and the month your Hermia goes live doesn't count.

What happens if I get more enquiries than my plan covers?

Hermia keeps answering every one of them. One busy month changes nothing: we let you know, and that's all.

If you're over your plan 4 months in a row, your plan moves up, to the plan that fits the quietest of those months. We email you the numbers and the list of every enquiry we counted, and the new monthly price starts on your first bill at least 7 days later. There's no setup fee for the move.

And if I get fewer?

If you're under your plan 4 months in a row, Hermia moves you down on your next bill, to the plan that fits the busiest of those 4 months. You don't have to ask, and we email you the numbers. Hermia works exactly the same; only the price changes.

Is there a limit on the Large plan?

No. Large has no upper limit and no custom quote. Every business with more than 2,500 enquiries a month is on Large, at USD 8,000 a month, and can choose it and start straight away.

Do I pay setup again when my plan changes?

No. Setup is paid once, for the plan you start on. Moving to another plan changes only your monthly fee, up or down.

Moving down doesn't refund setup you've already paid.

How do I know you counted right?

Every plan email shows your total for each month and where those enquiries came from, with a private link to the full list: each person's name, email or phone, the date and time, and the channel. The list opens only with your customer ID, and you can download it.

Names and contact details are deleted 6 months after each enquiry; the monthly totals are kept. If anything looks wrong, reply to the email and we'll go through it with you.

When does a new price start?

Always on a bill, never part-way through a month. A move up starts on your first bill at least 7 days after we email you. A move down starts on your next bill. Nothing is charged or credited for the month you're in.

How does the free trial work?

You pay your plan's setup fee at checkout and your Hermia is built, usually within 1 business day. Your 2-day free trial starts the day it's ready, not the day you pay, and your first monthly payment is taken when the 2 days end.

Cancel within the 2 days and the setup fee is refunded, less a USD 84 refund processing fee: USD 716 back on Starter, USD 4,316 on Standard, USD 7,416 on Growth and USD 14,916 on Large. Cancel before your Hermia is ready and the whole setup fee is refunded.

When exactly am I charged?

The setup fee is charged at checkout. The monthly fee is charged for the first time when your 2-day trial ends, and then every month after that. Your card is kept on file at checkout so the monthly fee can be taken.

Is there a minimum term? Can I cancel?

There's no minimum term. After the free trial it's month to month, and you can cancel any time. Service stops straight away when you cancel, and the rest of the month you've already paid for isn't refunded.

I run more than one business or brand. How does that work?

Each business or brand has its own Hermia, with its own setup and its own monthly fee, so each one's enquiries, questions and records stay separate.

Why does Hermia use only one channel to talk to my customers?

Hermia talks to your customers on one channel, WhatsApp or email, so every conversation stays in one place. Enquiries can still reach you from every way you connect. Changing the channel once you're live is a change request.

Are there any extra charges?

Only if you choose them: a change request once you are live, at USD 500 for each kind of change, on every plan. There are no usage charges: nothing per message, per channel, per question or per person who receives leads, and nothing for using (or not using) a CRM.

The only other amount on this page is the USD 84 refund processing fee, which applies only if you cancel during the free trial.

What counts as a change request?

Any change to your setup once it is live. There are three kinds, and each is USD 500:

Connections: adding or removing a way leads reach you, changing the channel customers are answered on, moving to a different CRM, or changing who receives leads.
Questions: adding, removing or rewording your questions, where each answer goes, your next step, or the reply language.
Calendar: how bookings work, approving or booking automatically, and which calendars are used.

Several changes of the same kind sent together are one charge. Going from five connections to two is USD 500; new questions and a calendar change together are USD 1,000. Reconnecting an account is always free, and your plan moves by itself, at no charge. You ask from the link we email you, and pay when you send the request.

What happens if a payment fails?

We email you straight away. If the account stays unpaid, Hermia pauses and stops replying to new enquiries, but your setup is kept. Pay, and it switches back on by itself. After 12 months unpaid your setup is removed; if you come back, it is built again from your saved configuration.

Do I need a CRM?

No, and the price is the same either way. If you use a CRM or job system that Hermia connects to, every finished enquiry goes straight into it. If you don't use one, or yours isn't connected yet, every enquiry still reaches you straight away as a secure lead docket by email.
