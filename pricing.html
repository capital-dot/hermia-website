The operating case

The intake layer that stops leads
leaking out of the pipeline.

Hermia gives the person approving the spend a practical answer: every genuine enquiry gets the same structured intake before a human needs to touch it. Fault details, access, hazards, availability, site confirmation, and customer context arrive ready for Simpro or AroFlo instead of being retyped from a voicemail the next morning.

“You’re not paying for a chatbot. You’re paying to stop losing jobs to slow response time — and to stop your best people doing data entry a system should be doing.”
01 / EVERY ENQUIRY

Consistent intake, every time.

The same questions and the same standard apply whether the enquiry arrives during business hours, after hours, or when the usual coordinator is unavailable. No variation in the quality of first response.

02 / USABLE BEFORE HAND-OFF

No vague message for the office to decode.

Hermia keeps qualifying an incomplete enquiry until the customer, site, issue, urgency and supporting detail are clear enough for your team to decide the next move. The team does not need to rebuild the story from an inbox thread.

03 / RESPONSE MOMENTUM

Faster response protects conversion.

When a genuine customer receives a qualified response promptly, the business keeps momentum while competing contractors are still waiting to reply. Speed becomes an operating advantage.

04 / CAPACITY

Scale without adding intake headcount.

As enquiry volume grows, the standard does not need to degrade. Hermia helps the business keep a controlled front door without immediately adding another person to keep up with every message.

05 / TRACEABILITY

A clear history behind every lead.

The enquiry, qualification detail, customer context, media, and handover remain connected. That gives management a stronger operational record for follow-up, internal review, disputes, and understanding where demand is actually coming from.
