Refund policy

2-day trial: the monthly service is trialled before the monthly charge begins.

After 48 hours, the setup fee is non-refundable. The USD $84 processing amount is non-refundable at all times.

Changes: any post-purchase change, rebuild, new route, migration, or retest starts at USD $500 plus the written implementation scope.
